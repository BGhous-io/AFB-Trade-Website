Real Estate

A home first. An asset second. Both done properly.

From newcomer first-home purchases to multi-unit commercial portfolios, we read the market, understand the financing, and represent only one side of the transaction — yours.

The Practice

A small brokerage with a long memory of the GTA.

Real estate is the largest cheque most of our clients will ever write. We treat it accordingly — with the same evidence-based caution we bring to an immigration file or a trade contract.

Our brokerage is licensed under the Real Estate Council of Ontario and operates primarily across the Greater Toronto Area — Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, and Oakville — with selected commercial coverage in Ottawa, Hamilton, and Calgary.

We do not list properties for the public. We work by referral and represent buyers, sellers, and landlords with whom we have an ongoing engagement letter. That model is deliberate — it lets us walk you away from a bad deal without worrying about a quarterly target.

What We Do

Six engagement types across the practice.

Newcomer Home Purchase

Your first home in Canada. Neighbourhood selection, mortgage pre-approval (including non-resident programs), and offer strategy.

Resale & Move-Up

Selling your current home and trading up. Staging, pricing strategy, marketing, and chain-managed closings.

Commercial & Plaza

Retail plazas, mixed-use buildings, and small-bay industrial. Underwriting, tenancy review, and capex modelling.

Investment & Portfolio

Multi-unit residential and small-portfolio acquisitions. Cap-rate analysis, financing structuring, and exit planning.

Property Management

Tenant placement, rent collection, maintenance coordination, and annual reporting. Fixed-fee or percentage models.

Leasing & Tenancy

Residential and commercial leasing. AGI applications, LTB representation, and lease renegotiation on owner instruction.
