International Trade

Moving goods between North America, Europe, and the Gulf.

Sourcing, contracting, freight forwarding, and customs — handled by a small team that has done this for two decades and answers in the same time zone as your buyer.

The Practice

End-to-end trade, run by people who have shipped before.

We are not a marketplace, a broker network, or a software platform. We are a small trade desk that takes responsibility for moving a container from the factory floor to your warehouse — and for everything that has to be true along the way.

Our clients typically come to us when their existing arrangement has failed: a supplier that ghosted, a customs file that stalled, or a contract that didn't survive contact with reality. We rebuild those relationships and run the operation properly — letters of credit, Incoterms that mean what you think they mean, and freight options priced honestly.

We operate primarily between Canadian and U.S. ports (Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Long Beach), the GCC (Jebel Ali, Hamad, Dammam), and selected European hubs (Rotterdam, Antwerp, Felixstowe).

How We Work

From RFQ to receipt, in four phases.

Brief

You describe the goods, the volumes, and the destination. We quote a delivered price and a realistic timeline within five working days.

Contract

We agree commercial terms — Incoterms, payment instrument, QC standard, and the named individuals responsible at each stage.

Execute

Production, inspection, booking, and customs filings. Daily status updates while goods are in motion; no news only when there is none.

Land & Reconcile

Receipt confirmed, documents archived, duty drawback claimed where available. Post-shipment review on every engagement.
